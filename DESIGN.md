# StreamForge UI

A light, quiet engineering workspace with warm orange actions and a cool green media surface. Manrope headings, DM Sans interface text, and IBM Plex Mono timestamps. Persistent sidebar on desktop; drawer on small screens. Borders carry structure; shadows stay subtle. Orange identifies primary actions and lab warnings; green marks healthy transport. Statuses always include labels.

Keep stream telemetry measured and explicitly label its hop and units. Keep synthetic video labeled as a test source. Do not introduce fabricated viewer counts, GPU graphs, latency measurements, or unavailable AI controls. Hardware-dependent features belong in the documented roadmap until implemented and tested.

The default source is a locally drawn and animated canvas landscape. It is real captured video sent through the SFU. No remote imagery is required. Typography uses web fonts with system fallbacks. Support reduced motion, focus outlines, labeled inputs, responsive layouts, and clear disabled/error states.
